Beyond the wake-up moment

BuzzBee looks after your whole night, not just the alarm.

Bedtime Reminder

A calming reminder and a dimmed breathing screen before your set bedtime — so winding down isn't an afterthought.

Calendar Auto-Shift

Checks tomorrow's first event and nudges you the evening before if your wake window would conflict with it.

Gentle Escalation

Volume ramps in gradually on an early wake — never an instant blast, unless the hard deadline leaves no more slack.

Record Your Own Alarm Sound

Your voice, a song clip, anything — record up to 15 seconds and use it instead of the built-in tones. Saved on your device only, never uploaded anywhere.

Everything stays on your phone.

No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync. BuzzBee's sensors are used live, on-device, and immediately discarded — never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

  • No account required to use the app
  • Microphone is read for volume level only — never recorded
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs
  • Your alarms and history never leave your device
